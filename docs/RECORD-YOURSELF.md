# Record it yourself — complete shot list

You record raw footage. I cut it, ramp the waits, burn in captions, insert the
architecture cards, mix the music and export for LinkedIn.

---

## Setup (5 minutes, do it properly)

- **OBS Studio** (free) or **Win + G**. 1920×1080, 60 fps, high bitrate.
- **Light mode.** Bookmarks bar off. Notifications off. Close every other tab.
- **Sign in first** so no login screen appears mid-take.
- Put one **strong room photo** on the Desktop.
- Start Colab, paste the tunnel, confirm the app answers — tell me the URL and
  I'll verify all three services before you press record.

### Two things that decide whether it looks human

1. **Move the mouse slowly, and pause before you click.** A cursor that stops,
   hovers, then clicks reads as a person. Mine teleported — that is most of why
   it felt robotic.
2. **Let things breathe.** After a result appears, sit on it for 3 seconds
   before moving. I can always cut time out; I cannot invent it.

**Record each block separately.** Stop between them. A fluff then costs you
30 seconds, not the whole take.

## The blocks

Numbers are rough targets. Longer is fine — I trim.

### 1 · Landing  (~60 s)
Scroll slowly through all five scenes. Pause on the one with labelled elements
and confidence values.

### 2 · Studio: upload  (~40 s)
Drag the photo in. Hover **each** culture card so its description shows —
Lebanese, Khaleeji, Moroccan — then choose **All three**.

### 3 · Generation  (record the whole wait)
Do not cut it short. Let "Inside DAR" play; I ramp it later. If you want to
talk, this is the moment.

### 4 · The reveal  (~60 s)
Drag the before/after divider **slowly**, twice, all the way each direction.
Then click through **Lebanese → Khaleeji → Moroccan**, pausing on each.

### 5 · All three tiles  (~25 s)
Scroll to the grid with Original + the three cultures. Hover a download button.

### 6 · Provenance X-ray  (~40 s)
Drag the scan line slowly across, both directions. Stop halfway and let the
labelled depth map sit on screen — this is one of your best shots.

### 7 · Understand  (~60 s)
Click 2–3 highlighted elements so the Arabic terms appear. Show the 2D plan.
Drag the depth orbit around.

### 8 · Paint the wall  (~50 s)
**Change colour** → **Wall** → pick a preset → **Preview** → **Confirm**.
Then do it again for **Floor** so both surfaces are shown.

### 9 · Furniture placement  (~50 s)
Scroll to the furniture panel. Pick a piece, position it, composite it in.

### 10 · The three story tabs  (~60 s)
**Design story**, **Culture DNA**, **Inside DAR** — open each, scroll a little.
Culture DNA is where the *verified / unverified* labels show. Linger there.

### 11 · Save + report  (~25 s)
**Save design**, then **Room report (PNG)** so the download appears.

### 12 · Build Mode entry  (~30 s)
**Design it yourself**. Stop for 3 seconds on the room that is already
populated from your photo. Point the cursor at the "N found" chip.

### 13 · Build Mode tour  (~90 s)
Catalogue rail — switch **Lebanese / Khaleeji / Moroccan** and let the pieces
change. Click one piece to open the **Inspector** (dimensions, material,
provenance). Show the **plan minimap**. Switch **Plan / Corner** view. Cycle
**Morning → Afternoon → Sunset → Night**.

### 14 · Placement rules  (~40 s)
Drag a sofa into a wall — hold there so the **red** refusal reads. Then drag it
onto a found object so the **amber** advisory shows, and drop it anyway.

### 15 · Undo / redo  (~20 s)
Ctrl+Z a whole AI plan away in one step, then redo it.

### 16 · The planner — four briefs  (each: type, wait, apply, pause)
1. `Seven guests are coming tonight. Make it a warm Lebanese living room.`
2. `Make it Moroccan.`
3. `Remove one chair.`
4. `Add a grand piano and an aquarium.`  ← it refuses; **let that sit on screen**

### 17 · Render with DAR  (~90 s)
**Finish**. Let the handoff panel sit — materials list, and the *what this can
and cannot hold* text. Point at the **depth** and **segmentation** thumbnails
for 3 seconds each. Then **Render with DAR**, record the whole wait, and when
the image lands: **right-click → Open image in new tab** and let it fill the
screen.

### 18 · Restyle  (~30 s)
Back in Build Mode, switch culture on the rail, then use **Restyle N** and let
the report of what it could not translate show.

### 19 · Reset  (~20 s)
The **Reset** button — show both options, then *Clear my design*.

### 20 · History & community  (~45 s)
`/history` — scroll your saved designs. Open one. Then `/others` — the gallery
and the ratings.

### 21 · Plan & usage  (~25 s)
`/subscription` — both plans, current weekly usage.

### 22 · Admin  (~40 s)
`/evaluation` — scroll it, and **pause on a cell reading "No data"**.
Then `/admin/users` and `/admin/subscriptions` briefly.

### 23 · Theme & language  (~30 s)
Toggle dark → light. Then **ع** for Arabic; scroll a little so the RTL mirroring
is obvious; toggle back.
