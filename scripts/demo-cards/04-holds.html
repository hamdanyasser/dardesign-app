Render with DAR

Normally it traces your photo.
Here it traces your 3D model.

The control images are rendered from the scene you arranged, so layout stops being something the model infers from a sentence.

Held

Placement, orientation, room geometry and viewpoint — because that is the control signal itself.

Not held

The surface of any individual piece. The model still invents ornament and texture inside the silhouette.

The app states this limit on screen, every time.
