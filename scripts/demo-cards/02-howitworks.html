How it works

It reads the room before it redesigns it.

1 · Your photoOne photograph of a real room.
2 · DepthHow far away every pixel is. This holds the geometry.
3 · SegmentationWhat each region is — wall, floor, sofa. This holds the layout.
4 · Cultural LoRAA model trained per culture. This holds the language.
5 · RenderSDXL generates only inside those constraints.

Tracing your room, not painting a new one.
