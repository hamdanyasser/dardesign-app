The language model

The LLM proposes. DAR decides.

You describe the room in a sentence. The model picks furniture and positions — and then six gates decide what may actually exist.

  • Closed vocabulary. Catalogue ids are a schema enum, so an invented piece is unrepresentable, not merely unlikely.
  • No dimensions. The model emits no sizes at all — they come from the ontology.
  • Validation. Bad ids, absurd coordinates and unknown materials are dropped and reported.
  • Collision. Every placement runs the same test a human drag gets.
  • Advisories pass. Judgement is not an error.
  • One room, one culture. Foreign pieces are removed and named.
